About Console Emulation

Understanding how software translates console hardware instructions into playable video games on modern systems.

Video Games & Hardware Emulation

I am Mohamed Aziz Thabet, currently studying in 1er année prepa tic at EPI. In one sentence, my passion is video games—both playing them and understanding how consoles work under the hood.

Console emulators (such as RPCS3, Dolphin, or CHIP-8 interpreters) act as virtual hardware layers. Instead of relying on physical console chips, an emulator decodes original CPU instructions, manages virtual memory banks, and translates graphics and audio commands so classic video games remain playable on modern computers.
